1960s–1970s

This section documents paintings and works on paper from Ron English’s early period, spanning the 1960s and 1970s.

Dates are shown when supported by signatures, inscriptions, archival documentation, or other available evidence. Works whose exact year remains uncertain are identified by the broadest date range currently supported by the record.

Grouped series, booklets, and notebooks appear once in this index. Their overview pages link to separately catalogued component works, each retaining its own Work ID.
