Notes

This work is catalogued from a surviving photocopy, which is the only known surviving version. The original drawing has not been located, and the photocopy should be understood as documentary evidence rather than the primary artwork. The photocopy was also used as a flyer for the Insane Ward party. Details of medium, surface, and exact scale may be revised if the original is found.
